Stillscreen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Stillscreen keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

Stillscreen (“the app”) is published by Gaurav kumar. You can contact us at gaurav954624@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The following is saved only on your phone:

  • The apps you choose to block, and your work apps for the wind-down
  • Your focus sessions (when they started and how long) and your streak
  • Your settings, such as whether you are a student or a working professional, strict mode, wind-down hours, and an exam name and date if you add one

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send it.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this app’s data in your Google backup. You control this in your phone’s settings.

Accessibility Service

If you agree, Stillscreen uses Android’s Accessibility Service to detect which app is currently in front. This lets it show a pause screen over the apps you chose to block, during focus sessions and your wind-down hours. If you turn on Mindful pause, it also shows a short breathing screen before apps you choose.

Stillscreen does not read what is on your screen, the text you type, or your passwords, and it does not share any of this. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

Mindful pause (optional)

If you turn on Mindful pause, Stillscreen shows a short pause before the apps you choose, at any time. It keeps simple counts on your phone, such as how many times an app paused you and how many times you chose not to continue, so it can show them to you. These counts never leave your phone and are removed after about a week.

Other permissions

Stillscreen can see which apps are installed on your phone. It uses this only to show you a list to choose from. The list stays on your phone.

Sharing your streak

If you tap Share on the stats screen, Stillscreen creates an image of your streak and hands it to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp or Instagram. This happens only when you choose it. Stillscreen itself does not send anything.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, Stillscreen asks for permission to show notifications and schedules one on your phone at the time you choose. The app can restart the reminder after your phone reboots. The reminder is created and shown on your phone only, and nothing about it is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

Accounts, ads and analytics

There is no account to create. The app shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

Children

Stillscreen is intended for people aged 13 and over. The app collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, for example by adding accounts or sync, this page and the date above will be updated before that version is released.
