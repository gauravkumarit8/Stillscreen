## What the app stores

The app stores the following only on your phone:
- The apps you choose to block
- Your focus sessions (start time, length) and streak
- Your settings (mode, strict mode, wind-down hours, exam name and date)

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account,
no advertising and no analytics, and it makes no network connections.

## Accessibility Service

If you agree, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect which app
is currently in front, so it can show a pause screen over apps you chose to
block. The app does not read what is on your screen, the text you type, or
your passwords, and does not send any of this anywhere. You can turn the
service off at any time in Android Settings.

## Permissions

- Accessibility Service: described above.
- Seeing which apps are installed: used only to show you a list to pick from.
  The list stays on your phone.
