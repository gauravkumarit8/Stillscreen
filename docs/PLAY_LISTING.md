## Full description (4000 characters max)

Put your phone down and get your work done.

Stillscreen is a free app blocker and focus timer. Pick the apps that pull you away, start a session, and Stillscreen steps in whenever you try to open them. Finish the timer, grow your streak, and watch your focus level climb.

No ads. No account. Nothing leaves your phone.

BLOCK DISTRACTIONS
- Choose the apps to block. One tap adds a whole group, like social media or video.
- Start a focus session of 25, 50, 90 minutes, or any length you like.
- A calm block screen replaces the app, so the urge passes instead of winning.
- Strict mode locks a session until the timer ends.
- Your phone's dialer and Settings are never blocked.

STAY MOTIVATED
- Daily streaks, with rest days you earn so one missed day does not erase your progress.
- Focus levels from Seed to Summit. Every minute counts toward the next one.
- A weekly recap, your best time of day to focus, and a heatmap of your last five weeks.
- Badges for streaks, long sessions and early starts.
- Pomodoro rounds with suggested short and long breaks.
- A daily reminder that stays quiet once you have reached your goal.

BUILT FOR STUDENTS
- Exam countdown, so you always know how many days you have left.
- Study sessions that fit a revision timetable, from quick bursts to long blocks.
- A mindful pause screen with a breathing moment before an app opens.

BUILT FOR WORK
- End-of-day wind-down: pause work apps like email and chat after hours.
- Choose your own hours and days.
- Deep-work sessions that keep you on one task.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No internet permission. Your sessions, streaks and settings stay on your phone.
- No ads and no tracking.
- You can share your streak as an image whenever you want, and only then.

HOW BLOCKING WORKS
Stillscreen uses Android's Accessibility Service for one purpose: to detect which app is open, so it can show a block screen over the apps you chose during a focus session. It does not read what is on your screen, what you type, or your passwords, and it does not send anything off your phone. You can turn it off at any time in Android settings.

KEEP BLOCKING RELIABLE
Some phones stop background apps to save battery. Stillscreen has a built-in guide and a blocking health check that shows whether blocking is running and how to fix it.

Preparing for board exams, entrance tests or a big deadline? Start a 25-minute session today and see how it feels to finish something without the scroll.

## Screenshots: tell a story, in this order

Use real screenshots from the app, never mock-ups. Play removes listings whose
screenshots do not match the app. Run `scripts/frame_screenshots.py` to add the
captions in `store/captions.txt`. One idea per screenshot:

1. Block screen: "Block distractions in one tap"
2. Home with the timer running: "Focus sessions that stick"
3. Stats with the focus level card: "Watch your focus level grow"
4. Weekly recap: "Your week at a glance"
5. Exam countdown: "Count down to exam day"
6. Wind-down: "Switch off after work"
7. Mindful pause: "A calm pause, not a lecture"
8. Privacy: "No ads. No account. Private."

The first two screenshots decide most installs, so make them the clearest.

## Ways to get more installs from the same listing

- **Store listing experiments** (Play Console, Grow): test the title, the short
  description, the icon and the first screenshot against each other. Change one
  thing at a time and wait for a clear result.
- **Custom store listings**: make one for each country, or for students and for
  people at work, with a matching first screenshot.
- **Translate the listing.** Even if the app is English only for now, a
  translated listing lets people search in their own language. Hindi first if
  many of your users are in India.
- **Ratings**: the in-app rating card asks after five finished sessions. Keep
  it that way, and reply to every review.
- **Android vitals**: keep crash and ANR rates low. They affect visibility.

## Do not

- Name other apps or brands in the title, description or tags.
- Repeat keywords or list exam names over and over. It looks like keyword
  stuffing and can get the listing rejected.
- Promise that blocking cannot be bypassed. Say "locked until the timer ends".
- Offer anything in return for a rating or review.
- Mention features that are not shipped yet (leaderboards, groups, Windows).
