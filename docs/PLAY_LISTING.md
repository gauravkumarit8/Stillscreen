## Full description

Stillscreen helps you put your phone down and get your work done.

Choose the apps that pull you away, start a focus session, and Stillscreen
shows a calm pause screen whenever you try to open them. Finish the timer and
you build a streak.

Made for students and for people at work. Pick your path when you start:

For students
- Exam countdown so you always know how many days you have left
- Study sessions of 25, 50 or 90 minutes
- Daily streaks and a simple weekly view of your focus time

For working professionals
- End-of-day wind-down: pause work apps like email and chat after hours
- Choose your own hours and days
- Deep-work sessions that block distracting apps

For everyone
- Strict mode: lock a session so you cannot end it early
- Protected essentials: the phone dialer and system settings are never blocked
- Private by design: your data stays on your phone

How it works
Stillscreen uses Android's Accessibility Service only to detect which app is
open, so it can show a pause screen for the apps you chose. It does not read
what is on your screen, what you type, or your passwords, and it does not send
anything off your phone. You can turn it off at any time in Android settings.

## Do not

- Name other apps or brands in the title, description or tags.
- Promise that blocking cannot be bypassed. Say "locked until the timer ends".
- Mention features that are not shipped yet (leaderboards, groups, Windows).
